# Design

## Visual Identity
A premium, editorial sports brand that balances traditional prestige with modern artistic precision.

## Colors
- **Core Neutral**: `oklch(100% 0.005 330)` (Soft white background)
- **Prestigious Black**: `oklch(15% 0.01 330)` (Deep ink for typography)
- **Chulalongkorn Pink**: `oklch(65% 0.22 355)` (Primary identity color, used for emphasis)
- **Subtle Stone**: `oklch(92% 0.005 330)` (Secondary neutral for subtle UI elements)

## Typography
- **Thai Serif**: `"Taviraj"`, `"Maitree"`, serif (Primary editorial voice)
- **Display**: `"Space Grotesk"`, sans-serif (Modern athletic technicality)
- **Serif Body**: `"Playfair Display"`, ui-serif, Georgia, serif (Legacy and prestige)
- **Sans Body**: `"Inter"`, ui-sans-serif, system-ui (Utility and readability)

## Spacing & Rhythm
- **Density**: Spacious/Editorial. Prioritize breathing room over content density.
- **Grids**: Asymmetric but balanced. Use large margins (max-w-7xl) and offset elements to create an artistic feel.

## Components
- **Borders**: Sharp 1px ink borders for structure. No rounded corners on main containers.
- **Imagery**: High-contrast, full-bleed photography. Use dark overlays to preserve legibility and drama.
- **Buttons**: Minimalist, high-contrast buttons with sharp edges and bold sans-serif labels.

## Motion
- **Entry**: Subtle `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` fade-ins with minimal vertical translation.
- **Energy**: Calm and deliberate. Avoid aggressive or bouncy animations.

## Do's
- Use bold serif headers in Thai for strong cultural identity.
- Leverage white space to create a "gallery" feel.
- Keep UI elements (nav, footers) minimalist to let content lead.

## Don'ts
- No rounded buttons or containers.
- No soft shadows or glassmorphism.
- Avoid cluttered grids; every section should have a clear focal point.
