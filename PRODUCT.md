# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Prospective student athletes, current squad members, university administrators, alumni, and official tournament organizers. They are looking for an authoritative, prestigious, and official record of the club's activities and roster.

## Product Purpose
To serve as the definitive official digital home for the Chulalongkorn University Golf Club. It must showcase the club's 100-year legacy ("The Pink Blazer"), current tournament results, and the active varsity squad with a high-end, editorial feel that matches the university's prestige.

## Brand Personality
Formal, editorial, and artistic. The site should feel like a premium sports magazine (e.g., The Golfer's Journal or high-end fashion editorials). It must balance modern performance with "Old World" prestige. Stylish but grounded in tradition.

## Anti-references
- AI-generated "SaaS" aesthetics (generic card grids, neon gradients, rounded "bubbly" buttons).
- Generic "Startup" templates.
- Overly cluttered or amateur sports blog layouts.
- "Glassmorphism" as a default decorative element.

## Design Principles
- **Editorial Authority**: Use bold, deliberate typography and generous white space to create a magazine-like experience.
- **Prestigious Pink**: Treat the Chulalongkorn pink as a mark of high distinction, not just a color—used with restraint and precision.
- **Artistic Precision**: Every alignment, border, and image placement must feel intentional and curated, like an art gallery.
- **Legacy First**: Modern features must never overshadow the 100-year history; technology should serve the tradition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for readability of editorial content.
- Respect for reduced motion in transitions.
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.
